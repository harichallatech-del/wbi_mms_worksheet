WBI MMS — EXTENSIONS AND TOOLS RESEARCH
Top 10 implementation TODOs
Research checked: 23 October 2026
Scope: the Manager, Stock Monitor and Operators workbooks; the supplied screen references; Google Sheets, Microsoft 365 Excel, forms, buttons, paginated lists, security, automation and dashboards.

EXECUTIVE RECOMMENDATION

Treat the workbook as a user interface and reporting surface. Keep the WBI API as the authority for records, identity and permissions. For the first operator pilot, choose one path: AppSheet if the team is Google-first, or Power Apps with Dataverse if the team is Microsoft-first and needs stronger offline support. Use Apps Script HTML Service or Office.js for the screenshot-style custom UI, and use a table library for richer search and paging. Do not let a third-party tool bypass the existing queued-write and WBI authorization path.

ARXIV AND RELATED RESEARCH

1. SpreadsheetBench 2: Evaluating Agents on End-to-End Business Spreadsheet Workflows (2026), arXiv:2606.29955.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29955
   Evaluates generation, debugging and visualization over multi-sheet business workbooks. The paper reports low end-to-end accuracy and very weak debugging accuracy for evaluated agents. WBI implication: use an AI assistant for drafts and explanations, not unsupervised formula, audit or production writes.

2. fxBench: Evaluating and Understanding Formula Suggestions in Spreadsheets (2026), arXiv:2610.24729.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24729
   Introduces 503 real spreadsheet formula-suggestion tasks; the best tested configuration reached 58.8% correct suggestions. WBI implication: inspect and approve formula suggestions; do not silently accept generated KPI or stock formulas.

3. Auditing and Controlling AI Agent Actions in Spreadsheets (2026), arXiv:2604.20070.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20070
   Studies Pista, an agent design that exposes actions for user oversight. WBI implication: prefer reviewable, stepwise queue actions with visible before/after values and explicit approval.

4. FinSheet-Bench: From Simple Lookups to Complex Reasoning, Where LLMs Break on Financial Spreadsheets (2026), arXiv:2603.07316.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07316
   Finds spreadsheet reasoning degrades as workbook structure and complexity grow, and argues against unsupervised model use for professional numeric work. WBI implication: derive OEE/stock totals deterministically and reconcile summary cards to source records.

5. SpreadsheetBench: Towards Challenging Real World Spreadsheet Manipulation (2024), arXiv:2406.14991; NeurIPS 2024.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14991
   Uses 912 real spreadsheet questions and tests realistic layouts, multiple tables and non-textual structure. WBI implication: benchmark tools on the actual WBI role workbooks and screenshots, not on toy sheets.

6. SpreadsheetLLM: Encoding Spreadsheets for Large Language Models (2024), arXiv:2407.09025.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09025
   Shows that spreadsheet structure, anchors and formats matter when a model reads a sheet. WBI implication: preserve clear headers, named tables, normalized source tables and role maps so automation has reliable context.

7. ExceLint: Automatically Finding Spreadsheet Formula Errors (OOPSLA 2018; paper indexed by Microsoft Research).
   https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/excelint-automatically-finding-spreadsheet-formula-errors/
   Uses formula-pattern anomalies to flag potential errors. WBI implication: formula linting can be a useful warning layer, but it is not a substitute for tests, review or WBI API reconciliation.

RESEARCH LIMITS AND ADOPTION NOTES

- This is a fit-ranked shortlist, not a paid-product endorsement. Verify current tenant licensing, regional availability, data residency, privacy terms, security review and support before procurement.
- Vendor product pages can change. The source links above are direct vendor documentation/product pages; research links point to arXiv or the publisher/research lab.
- The most important architecture decision is where writes and authorization live. Add-ons, Apps Script, low-code apps, and buttons improve the UI; they do not replace WBI's server-side permissions.
- The studies evaluate particular agents/benchmarks. Their results inform cautious design; they are not a direct performance test of this WBI project.
